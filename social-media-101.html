Social Media Marketing 101: Choosing the Right Platform for Your Brand

2026-02-175 min readBranket Solutions

The most common social media mistake isn't posting the wrong thing — it's trying to be active on every platform at once, and ending up mediocre on all of them instead of good on one or two.

Start with where your customers already are

A B2B consulting business and a home bakery don't belong on the same platforms with the same energy. Before picking a platform, it helps to ask plainly: where does this specific audience already spend time, and what are they doing there — browsing, researching, or just killing time?

Visual, product-based businesses

Instagram tends to reward businesses that have something worth looking at — food, fashion, interiors, handmade goods, before/afters. If what you sell photographs well, this is usually where to put the first real effort.

Local and community-driven businesses

Facebook still carries weight for local discovery, reviews, and community groups — especially for services people ask their neighbors about.

B2B and professional services

LinkedIn works better for businesses selling to other businesses or professionals, where credibility and expertise matter more than aesthetics.

Consistency beats frequency

Posting five times a week for two weeks and then going quiet for a month teaches an algorithm — and your audience — to stop expecting anything from you. A realistic, sustainable schedule posted consistently outperforms a burst of enthusiasm that fades.

What actually works over time

  • Content built around real questions customers ask, not generic "motivational" posts
  • Behind-the-scenes glimpses that make the business feel real, not just promotional
  • A clear, simple call to action — message us, visit the link, call now
  • Paid boosts used to amplify content that's already working, not to rescue content that isn't

The goal isn't to "be on social media." It's to be in front of the specific people who'd actually buy from you, on the platform where they're already paying attention.
